Normal forms

Each normal form includes the previous one

1NF

Atomic values

2NF

No partial deps

3NF

No transitive deps

BCNF

Every determinant is a key

4NF

No multi-valued deps

5NF

No join deps

Practical designs usually stop at 3NF or BCNF. Denormalization deliberately goes back down for performance.
