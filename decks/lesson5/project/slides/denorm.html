Normal forms

Denormalization

What

Deliberately storing the join of normalized tables, or a derived value, as a base table (a lower normal form).

When

  • Read-heavy reports and dashboards
  • Data warehouses (star schemas)
  • Measured performance problems, not guesses

Cost

  • Redundancy comes back
  • Application or triggers must keep copies in sync
  • More storage

Normalize first; denormalize only with a measured reason, and document it.
