Suppliers

Suppliers data

  • Relation (S#, Country, Currency, P#, Qty)
  • S#: supplier number
  • Country: where the supplier is located
  • Currency: the currency of that country
  • P#: part number supplied
  • Qty: quantity supplied to date

Key

Qty depends on supplier and part, so the PK is the composite {S#, P#}.

  • S# → Country
  • Country → Currency
  • {S#, P#} → Qty
