Normal forms

Third normal form (3NF)

A table is in 3NF if it is in 2NF and no non-key attribute depends transitively on the primary key (no non-key → non-key dependency).

To reach 3NF

  • Move the dependent non-key attributes to a new table
  • Its PK is the non-key attribute they depend on
  • Leave that attribute in the original table as a FK

Every non-key attribute must depend on the key, the whole key, and nothing but the key.
