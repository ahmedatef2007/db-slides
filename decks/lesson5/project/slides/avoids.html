Why normalize

Normalization avoids

Duplicated data

The same fact stored in many rows.

Insert anomaly

Can't record one fact without another.

Update anomaly

One change must be made in many places.

Delete anomaly

Deleting one fact loses another.

Frequent NULLs

Wasted space, unclear meaning.

When to use it

  • To certify the quality of a relational design
  • When taking over designs from legacy systems, files or spreadsheets

How far to go

  • Usually 3NF or BCNF in practice
  • Higher forms (4NF, 5NF) for special cases
