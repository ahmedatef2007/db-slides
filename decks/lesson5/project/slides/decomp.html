Good decompositions

Lossless join (required)

Joining the new tables gives back exactly the original rows: no spurious rows.

  • Split R into R1, R2 so the common columns are a key of R1 or R2
  • That is why we keep the FK (Level) in STUDENT

Dependency preservation (desired)

Every FD can still be checked inside one table.

  • 3NF can always keep both properties
  • BCNF sometimes must give this up (TEACH example)
