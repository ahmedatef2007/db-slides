Beyond BCNF: 4NF and 5NF

4NF: multi-valued dependencies

Two independent multi-valued facts about the same key in one table.

  • EMP(Ename, Pname, Dname): projects and dependents are unrelated
  • Every project × every dependent → huge redundancy
  • Fix: EMP_PROJECTS(Ename, Pname) + EMP_DEPENDENTS(Ename, Dname)

5NF: join dependencies

A table that can only be rebuilt by joining three or more of its projections.

  • Rare in practice
  • Related to the ternary vs three binaries question (Lesson 3)

A well-designed ERD mapped with the 7 steps rarely produces 4NF/5NF problems: multi-valued attributes already get their own tables.
