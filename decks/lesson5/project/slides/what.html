Why normalize

What is normalization?

Definition: Normalization takes a table through a series of tests (normal forms) to certify the goodness of a design and minimize redundancy and anomalies.

Top-down design

Conceptual model first (ERD), then map to tables (Lessons 2–3).

  • Normalization checks the result
  • Most common in practice

Bottom-up design

Start from attributes and their relationships (e.g. existing forms, files, spreadsheets).

  • Normalization builds the tables
  • Typical for legacy data
