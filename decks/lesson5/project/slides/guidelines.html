Why normalize

Four informal design guidelines

1

Clear semantics

Don't combine attributes from different entities or relationships in one table.

2

No anomalies

Design tables so insert, update and delete anomalies cannot occur.

3

Few NULLs

Attributes that are often NULL belong in a separate table (with the PK).

4

No spurious tuples

Join only on (FK, PK) pairs; joining on other matching columns invents rows.
