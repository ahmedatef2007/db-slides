Tel is multi-valued: one student can have several phone numbers. It stands alone, so it moves to its own table.

Subject is multi-valued too, but each subject brings its own Subj_Desc and Grade: the three form a repeating group.

Level → Level_Mgr and Subject → Subj_Desc are FDs hidden inside the table: they drive the 2NF and 3NF steps.
