Method

From narrative to ERD, step by step

1

Find entities

Nouns that have their own facts: department, project

2

Find attributes

Facts about a noun: name, number, location

3

Pick keys

Words like unique, identified by

4

Find relationships

Verbs linking nouns: controls, works on

5

Cardinality

one, several, many, a number of

6

Participation

must → total; may → partial

7

Weak entities & extras

No own key? Relationship attributes like hours?

8

Review with users

Read every relationship aloud as a sentence
