Case study 1

Car manufacturer (2/2)

Each model must be produced at just one of the firm's factories, which are located in London, Birmingham, Bristol, Wolverhampton and Manchester (one in each city). Each factory has a number of machines, capacity, and computer system used (OS, DBMS, Internet).

A factory produces many models of cars and many types of parts.

Think: Is the key of MODEL just Name, or (Name, Suffix)? Is images single- or multi-valued? Is computer system composite?
