Modeling basics

Entities, instances, attributes

Entity

A thing in the real world with an independent existence.

  • Physical: a person, a car
  • Conceptual: a job, a course

Entity instance

One particular occurrence of an entity.

  • Mai, Ali are instances of STUDENT

Attribute

A property that describes the entity.

  • EMPLOYEE: name, age, address, salary
