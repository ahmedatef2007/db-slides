Common ERD mistakes

Attributes as entities

City with only a name is usually an attribute, not an entity.

Foreign keys in the ERD

Don't add DeptNo to EMPLOYEE: the relationship already says it. FKs appear in Lesson 3.

Wrong side for cardinality

Read both directions aloud as sentences before writing 1 or N.

Relationship facts on an entity

Hours on EMPLOYEE is wrong: it depends on the project too.

Storing derived values

Prefer BirthDate over Age; mark derived attributes dashed.

Ignoring history

"Keep track of changes" means a relationship with dates, not one overwritten value.
