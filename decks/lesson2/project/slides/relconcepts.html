Relationships

Describing a relationship

A relationship is an association between entity instances: Ali works for Research.

How many?

Degree

How many entity types take part: unary, binary, ternary…

At most?

Cardinality ratio

The maximum number of instances an entity can be related to: 1:1, 1:N, M:N.

At least?

Participation

The minimum: must every instance take part (total) or not (partial)?
