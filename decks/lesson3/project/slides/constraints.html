Relational model

Integrity constraints

PRIMARY KEY

Unique + not null. Identifies each row. Entity integrity: no part of a PK may be NULL.

UNIQUE

No two rows share the value (an alternate key). NULL usually allowed.

NOT NULL

A value is mandatory: total participation often becomes NOT NULL.

FOREIGN KEY

Value must exist in the referenced PK, or be NULL. Referential integrity.

CHECK

A condition every row must satisfy: Salary > 0, Gender IN ('M','F').
