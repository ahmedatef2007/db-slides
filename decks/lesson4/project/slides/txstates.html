New diagram (state diagram from Elmasri & Navathe, chapter 20). A transaction starts Active, becomes Partially committed after its last operation, and Committed once its changes are safely recorded. Any failure moves it to Failed, after which the DBMS rolls it back (Aborted) so the database returns to its state before the transaction.
