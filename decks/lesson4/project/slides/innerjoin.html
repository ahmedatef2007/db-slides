Inner join: two syntaxes

Retrieve the name and address of employees who work for the Research department.

Older style (join condition in WHERE)

SELECT fname, lname, address

FROM   employee, department

WHERE  dname = 'Research'

  AND  dnumber = dno;

ANSI JOIN syntax (recommended)

SELECT e.fname, e.lname, e.address

FROM   employee e

JOIN   department d ON d.dnumber = e.dno

WHERE  d.dname = 'Research';

The ANSI form keeps join conditions (ON) apart from filters (WHERE) and works the same for outer joins. Forgetting the condition in the old style silently creates a Cartesian product.
