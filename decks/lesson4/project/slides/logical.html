AND, OR, NOT and precedence

SELECT ssn, fname, salary

FROM   employee

WHERE  salary > 5000

  AND  title NOT LIKE '%manager';

 

-- AND binds tighter than OR:

WHERE  salary > 5000 AND salary < 10000

   OR  title NOT LIKE '%manager'

 

-- say what you mean with parentheses:

WHERE  salary > 5000

  AND (salary < 10000 OR title NOT LIKE '%manager')

Precedence

  • NOT
  • AND
  • OR

The two last WHERE clauses return different rows. Use parentheses whenever you mix AND and OR.
