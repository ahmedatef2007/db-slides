Equi-join and non-equi-join

  • Equi-join: condition uses = (d.dnumber = e.dno)
  • Non-equi-join: any other operator (BETWEEN, <, >=)

SELECT e.name, e.salary, j.grade

FROM   employees  e

JOIN   job_grades j

  ON   e.salary BETWEEN j.low_sal AND j.high_sal;
