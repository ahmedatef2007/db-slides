Views

Simple vs complex views

FeatureSimple viewComplex view
Number of tablesOneOne or more
Contains functionsNoYes
Contains groups of dataNoYes
DML through the viewYesNot always

You can INSERT, UPDATE or DELETE through a simple view; complex views (joins, GROUP BY, DISTINCT, aggregates) are usually read-only.
