Self join: employees and their supervisors

SELECT e.fname AS employee,

       s.fname AS supervisor

FROM   employee e

JOIN   employee s ON e.super_ssn = s.ssn;

 

-- full names: e.fname || ' ' || e.lname (standard, Oracle, PostgreSQL)

-- CONCAT(e.fname, ' ', e.lname) (SQL Server, MySQL)

Two aliases of the same table play two roles: e = employee, s = supervisor.

Swap the condition (s.super_ssn = e.ssn) and the roles swap: you would list supervisors as employees.

Use LEFT JOIN to also list employees with no supervisor.
