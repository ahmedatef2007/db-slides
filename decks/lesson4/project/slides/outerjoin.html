Outer join: keep unmatched rows

Display all departments, even those with no employees.

SELECT e.name  AS employee,

       d.id    AS dept_id,

       d.name  AS department

FROM   employees e

RIGHT  JOIN departments d

       ON e.dept_id = d.id;
