Window functions: aggregates without collapsing rows

Keep every employee row and show their rank and the department average.

SELECT dno, fname, salary,

       RANK() OVER (PARTITION BY dno

                    ORDER BY salary DESC) AS rank_in_dept,

       AVG(salary) OVER (PARTITION BY dno) AS dept_avg

FROM   employee;
