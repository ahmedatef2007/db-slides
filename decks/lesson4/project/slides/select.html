Anatomy of a query

SELECT   dno, COUNT(*) AS n      -- 5

FROM     employee                -- 1

WHERE    salary > 20000          -- 2

GROUP BY dno                     -- 3

HAVING   COUNT(*) > 2            -- 4

ORDER BY n DESC;                 -- 6

Logical order

  • 1 FROM: pick the table(s)
  • 2 WHERE: filter rows
  • 3 GROUP BY: form groups
  • 4 HAVING: filter groups
  • 5 SELECT: compute columns
  • 6 ORDER BY: sort
