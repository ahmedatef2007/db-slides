Subqueries

Correlated subqueries and EXISTS

-- suppliers who have at least one order

SELECT *

FROM   suppliers s

WHERE  EXISTS (SELECT 1

               FROM   orders o

               WHERE  o.supplier_id = s.supplier_id);

 

-- employees with no dependents

SELECT fname

FROM   employee e

WHERE  NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1

                   FROM   dependent d

                   WHERE  d.essn = e.ssn);

Correlated

The inner query refers to the outer row (s.supplier_id), so it is evaluated once per outer row.

EXISTS

TRUE as soon as the subquery returns at least one row; the selected columns don't matter.
